# Complete Register of UK Ministerial Meetings Data Sources
## Current and Historical Collections for All Departments

**Last Updated:** January 2026  
**Purpose:** Comprehensive directory of all published ministerial meetings with external organisations/individuals

---

## IMPORTANT CONTEXT

### Policy Changes

**July 2024 Change:** From 5 July 2024, the Ministerial Code mandated that gifts and hospitality be published centrally by the Cabinet Office. Individual departments now only publish:
- Meetings with external organisations/individuals
- Overseas travel

**Pre-July 2024:** Departments published all four categories together (meetings, travel, gifts, hospitality)

### Data Format
- **Current Standard:** CSV files (UTF-8 encoding) - mandatory since April 2024
- **Historical:** Mix of CSV, XLS, XLSX, PDF
- **Publication Frequency:** Quarterly (approximately 3 months after quarter ends)

## ACCESSING THE DATA

### By Collection Page
Most departments maintain a collection page that lists all quarterly publications in reverse chronological order. These are the most reliable access points.

### By Individual Publication
For departments without collection pages (DCMS, Scotland Office, NI Office, AGO, UKEF), use the organisation search:

1. Go to https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/[department-name]
2. Click "Publications"
3. Filter by "Transparency data"
4. Search for quarterly returns by date range

### Via data.gov.uk
All transparency data is also mirrored on data.gov.uk:
- https://www.data.gov.uk
- Search: "ministerial transparency [department name]"

### Data Format Notes
- **2010-2014:** Mix of PDF, XLS, XLSX
- **2014-2024:** Primarily XLSX, with increasing CSV adoption
- **April 2024 onwards:** Mandatory CSV (UTF-8)

## SEARCH STRATEGIES

### For Comprehensive Historical Research

**Step 1:** Start with the oldest data
- DfT (2009 archives)
- Cabinet Office, DfE, DWP, Defra (2010)

**Step 2:** Track departmental reorganisations
- BIS → BEIS → DBT/DESNZ/DSIT
- DECC → BEIS → DESNZ
- FCO + DFID → FCDO
- DCLG → MHCLG → DLUHC → MHCLG

**Step 3:** Account for name changes in search
- Search both old and new department names
- Check URLs - many retained old acronyms

**Step 4:** Use data.gov.uk for discovery
- Often easier to find historical datasets
- Provides direct download links

### For Current Data

**Best Practice:**
1. Check the collection page first (if it exists)
2. Look for most recent quarterly publication
3. Download CSV files where available
4. Cross-reference with central gifts/hospitality register (post July 2024)

## IMPORTANT NOTES

### What is NOT Included

**Non-Ministerial Departments** do not publish ministerial transparency data because they are headed by civil servants rather than ministers. These include:
- HMRC
- Ofsted  
- CPS
- Food Standards Agency
- 20 other non-ministerial departments

### What Meetings are Excluded

Per Ministerial Code guidance, the following are NOT published:
- Meetings with other government departments/NDPBs/agencies
- Meetings with MPs/Lords in their parliamentary capacity
- Meetings with devolved or foreign governments (unless external org also present)
- Constituency meetings (unless impact ministerial role)
- Classified/exempt meetings (FOI exemptions apply)

### Coverage Gaps

Some departments have gaps in historical data:
- DCMS: 2014-2020 data exists but not in collection (must search individually)
- MoD: Annual publication until 2023 (less granular than quarterly)
- Territorial Offices: Sporadic publication in early years

### Data Quality Notes

- Early data (2010-2013): Often less detailed, format inconsistencies
- 2013-2020: Increasingly standardised
- 2020-Present: High quality, consistent CSV format
- Post-April 2024: Fully standardised CSV (UTF-8)
