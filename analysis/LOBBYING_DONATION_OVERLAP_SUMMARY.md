## Key Takeaways

1. **Lobbying and donations are MOSTLY SEPARATE activities**
   - 46,944 lobbying consultancies exist
   - Only 62 organizations (0.4%) both lobby AND donate
   - Most companies lobby WITHOUT donating (pharma, energy, tobacco)

2. **Trade unions dominate the overlap**
   - 3 trade unions = 93.4% of lobbying-client donation value
   - Unite alone = 88% (£269M)
   - 45 companies combined = 6.2% (£18.9M)

3. **Political parties, not MPs, receive the bulk**
   - Parties get 92% of lobbying-client donations
   - Individual MPs get only 3.4%
   - Most goes to Labour Party via Unite

4. **Foreign influence is real but small**
   - Cayman Islands + Qatar = £965K (0.3% of lobbying-client donations)
   - But raises serious conflict-of-interest questions

5. **Peak donation years align with elections**
   - 2019 (£55M), 2017 (£48M), 2015 (£27M) - all election years
   - Unite backs left-wing Labour leadership candidates

6. **21 MPs have broader industry connections**
   - Receive from 2+ different lobbying clients
   - Higher potential for policy capture vs. single-donor MPs
